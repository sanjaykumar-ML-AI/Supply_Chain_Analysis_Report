#**Project Title: Supply_Chain_Analysis_Report**
This project demonstrates End to End Supply Chain Managemnet and helps in translating complex supply chain data into actionable business intelligence  using interactive dashboards and analytics.
This Power BI Report Gives Insights about supply chain management to make sure right peoducts reaches right store or WH at right time which in terms increases the operational efficiency and Revenue as well 

Domain: Retail Logistics / Supply Chain Management

Tools: Power BI, DAX, Advanced Excel

**Executive Summary**
Developed a strategic multi-page analytics suite for Supply chain Management Team to oversee 36K Units of orders and a $82.7M revenue stream. This project focuses on identifying bottlenecks in fulfillment, and optimizing regional inventory distribution across a catalog of  367 Stores and 47 Products.

**Project Overview**
Developed an interactive Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard using Power BI to monitor sales, profits, warehouse performance, and delivery efficiency. The dashboard helps in identifying operational trends and business growth opportunities.

**Sales & Profit Analysis**
Analyzed $82.69M in total sales and $30.87M in profits across multiple sales channels. Built KPI-driven visualizations to track revenue growth, profitability, and monthly sales trends.


**Warehouse & Delivery Insights**
Evaluated warehouse performance based on orders, revenue contribution, and fulfillment efficiency. Monitored average delivery timelines to improve operational performance and customer satisfaction.


**Sales Channel Performance**
Compared In-Store, Online, Distributor, and Wholesale channels to identify top-performing revenue sources. Generated insights into customer purchasing patterns and channel profitability.


**Discount Impact Analysis**
Analyzed how discount categories affect revenue and profit margins. Helped identify balanced pricing strategies for maximizing profitability.


**Tools & Technologies**
Used Power BI, DAX, and data transformation techniques to create dynamic dashboards and interactive reports. Implemented filters, slicers for enhanced user experience.

**Data Cleaning & Consistency**
Handling missing values, inconsistent warehouse codes, and duplicate records was a major challenge. Data standardization was required to ensure accurate KPI calculations and reporting.


**KPI & DAX Calculations**
Creating dynamic KPIs such as profit margins, delivery efficiency, and discount impact involved complex DAX measures and calculations.


**Insight Generatio**n
Transforming raw supply chain data into meaningful business insights required careful analysis of trends, sales behavior, and operational efficiency metrics.

**Top Recommendations**

**Optimize Low-Performing Warehouses**
Focus on improving fulfillment efficiency and reducing delays in warehouses with lower operational performance.

**Improve Delivery Timelines**
Implement route optimization and logistics monitoring to reduce average delivery days and improve customer experience.

**Refine Discount Strategy**
Avoid excessive discounts on low-margin products and apply targeted discounts based on sales performance.

**Strengthen Online Sales Channel**
Invest more in online sales and digital marketing to increase revenue contribution from growing customer segments.

**Monitor High-Performing Products**
Maintain inventory availability for top-selling products to avoid stock shortages and missed revenue opportunities.

**Implement Predictive Analytics**
Use forecasting models for demand prediction, inventory planning, and proactive supply chain management.

**Build Real-Time Monitoring**
Integrate real-time supply chain tracking for faster operational visibility and quicker business response.
